The Problem

When Disaster Strikes, Information Saves Lives

  • Every hazard's official data lives on a different portal, in a different format — fire, flood, cyclone, landslide and cold wave never appear together.
  • Warnings are published in technical language and rarely reach people in their own language.
  • Old news is forwarded as if it were happening now — creating panic in one place and false calm in another.
  • There is no single place that shows what is happening across India, right now, from verified sources.

The Idea

One Portal Opens Five Dedicated Websites

EarthPulse India is a live disaster-information platform for India. A single portal opens five dedicated websites — one per hazard — each with its own live data, maps, verified history and assistant.

The Living Globe

The Globe Shows Only What Is Happening Now

  • Live only. The home globe displays current events — for fire, only the last 24 hours of satellite detections.
  • Calm means calm. When nothing is happening, the globe stays quiet. Old records never appear on it.
  • Honest about time. Every live marker carries its date and time, and fades as it grows older.
  • History has its own place. The past lives on a separate 2D map — never mixed into the live view.

Trusted Sources

Where the Data Comes From

Every layer of EarthPulse India begins with an official source — satellites in orbit and India's own monitoring agencies.

Forest Fire · From Space

NASA FIRMS

Satellite fire hotspots from the VIIRS and MODIS instruments, filtered to India and cross-checked between sensors.

Flood

Central Water Commission

Official river-level bulletins — which stations are above the warning level, and which have reached danger level.

Cyclone

IMD / RSMC New Delhi

India Meteorological Department cyclone bulletins, forecast tracks and landfall guidance for both coasts.

Landslide

Geological Survey of India

GSI landslide forecasts for hill districts, combined with IMD rainfall — the trigger behind most landslips.

Cold Wave

IMD Warnings

India Meteorological Department cold-wave warnings and observed minimum temperatures.

Historical Archive

Verified, One by One

Every historical record — all 275 — was checked against its original published source before it was added.

The honesty rule: every record panel carries a “Source — read the original report” button. Event media is credited — or honestly noted when none exists. Nothing is borrowed from another event, and nothing is faked.

Accounts & Scale

Sign Up Once. Your Account Works Everywhere.

One account opens the portal on any device — phone, laptop or desktop — and stays signed in. Here is exactly where a million users' data lives:

🔐 Firebase Authentication

Sign-up and login with email and password, run by Google's identity platform. Password reset arrives by email — no manual support needed.

🗄️ Cloud Firestore — Mumbai Region

Each user's profile is stored as a document in Google's Cloud Firestore database, in the asia-south1 (Mumbai) region — close to Indian users, fast everywhere.

🛡️ Private by Design

Firestore security rules guarantee that every user can read and write only their own account data. Accounts stay private to their owners — even with millions of users.

📈 Built to Scale

The same Google cloud infrastructure serves a hundred users or millions without any redesign — and the platform starts on the free tier, at zero cost.

Why It Matters

In a disaster, minutes decide outcomes — and minutes are lost when people must hunt through five portals, three languages and last year's forwarded warnings.

  • One trusted place — every Indian, every hazard, always current, always sourced.
  • Truth over noise — live means live: dated, verified, and quiet when nothing is happening.
  • Built from space data — satellite eyes on India's forests and coasts, translated into language people actually speak.
